# Data Analyst (Lisbon, hybrid)

A mid-sized logistics company in Lisbon is hiring a Data Analyst to support its operations team.

## Mandatory requirements
- Degree in a quantitative field (statistics, economics, engineering, computer science or similar)
- At least 2 years of professional experience in data analysis
- Proficiency in SQL and in Python or R
- Fluent Portuguese and English

## Desirable requirements
- Experience building dashboards (Power BI, Tableau or Looker)
- Familiarity with A/B testing and basic statistical modelling
- Experience in logistics, retail or supply chain
